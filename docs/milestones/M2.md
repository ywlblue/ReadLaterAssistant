## What I learned

- **Distance is not evidence.** q17 (no answer, 0.585) is closer than q10 (has an answer, 0.590).
  No threshold separates them. The fix has to look at the passages, not the score.
- **Lexical alone cannot refuse.** It always finds some keyword. Hybrid must keep the dense distance.
- **A dev set at the ceiling cannot pick a winner.** Hybrid's MRR lead is 2 cases. Not enough to add
  an extra index to maintain.
- **Check the eval tooling too.** A 600-char cut in the audit sheet made 4 good answers look bad.
- **Some "misses" are label problems** (q24). Fix labels by reading the article, and log the change.
